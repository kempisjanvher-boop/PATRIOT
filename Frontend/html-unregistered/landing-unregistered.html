Through Film and Solidarity, "Sineng Kalye" Amplifies the Voices of Farmers and Fisherfolk

Via Soderev Renz Silva

RAGE! for Systematic Change held "Sineng Kalye" at the Pavilion, Sucat Brgy. Hall, Muntinlupa City on October 26, 2025, to celebrate Peasant Month. The event featured five indie films portraying the real struggles of farmers and fisherfolk in the Philippines. It also included a discussion on corruption and social issues, led by young leaders advocating for truth and justice. Through art and unity, the event reminded everyone that the youth and the masses can create change together.
